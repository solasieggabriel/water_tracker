# 💧 HydroPulse — Personal Water Tracker

A modern, mobile-first progressive web app designed to track your daily hydration directly from your phone.

🔗 **Live App (GitHub Pages):** [https://solasieggabriel.github.io/water_tracker/](https://solasieggabriel.github.io/water_tracker/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Daily Intake Tracking**: Track in mL and percentage towards your customizable daily goal (e.g., 2,000 mL).
- **Realistic Glass Graphic**: Crystal-clear cup with animated water levels, sloshing waves, and reflections.
- **Timer Since Last Glass (`HH:MM`)**: Running timer measuring elapsed time since your last finished glass.
- **2-Hour Hydration Alarm**: Automatically alerts you with an audible chime (Web Audio API) and visual banner when 2 hours pass without a drink.
- **Finish & Refill Lifecycle**: Realistic glass state where the cup must be drunk before refilling, and the cabinet unlocks only when empty.
- **Glasses Cabinet**: Add, edit, remove, and equip different vessels (e.g., Standard Glass 250 mL, Coffee Mug 330 mL, Hydro Flask 500 mL) while preserving your daily total.
- **4:00 AM Circadian Day Reset**: Daily tracker resets at 4:00 AM instead of midnight so late-night drinks count toward the proper day.
- **Slideable & Full Monthly Calendar**: Scroll horizontally across recent days or open the full monthly history calendar to review previous intake and goals.
- **Quick Undo**: Undo accidental clicks with one tap (`↺`) to revert logged water and refill the glass.
- **Goal Reached Celebration**: Full-screen confetti and celebration chime when you hit 100% of your daily goal.
- **Light Theme**: Clean, refreshing aquatic aesthetic tailored for phone screens.
